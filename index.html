Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Seytrons.

What is Seytrons?

Seytrons is a free Android privacy and security app. It blocks intrusive ads, prevents DNS and IPv6 leaks, and helps keep malicious sites away.

Is Seytrons really free?

Yes. Seytrons is free to download and use. There is no account, no subscription, and no premium tier.

Which devices does Seytrons support?

Seytrons runs on Android phones and tablets. An iOS version is in development and will be announced here first.

Does Seytrons work in Kampala?

Yes. Seytrons is built in Kampala, Uganda, and tested against local mobile and WiFi networks.

How does Seytrons block ads?

Seytrons filters advertising and tracking requests before they load, which removes intrusive ads and speeds up page loading.

Who makes Seytrons?

Seytrons is developed by Twale James and the AlpherTech Solutions team in Kampala.
